We are running an ongoing VLA Large Program, based on project 25A-330, _A Centimeter-band Continuum Survey of Taurus Protoplanetary Disks_.  A continuation was proposed for that work (26A-251), and those programs will be supplemented with higher frequency observations with ALMA (2025.1.00321.S) and potentially the SMA (pending proposal).
