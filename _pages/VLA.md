We are running an ongoing VLA Large Program, based on project 25A-330, 

_A Centimeter-band Continuum Survey of Taurus Protoplanetary Disks_.  

A continuation was proposed for that work (26A-251), and those programs will be supplemented with higher frequency observations with ALMA (2025.1.00321.S) and potentially the SMA (pending proposal).  The current observations include X and Ku-band measurements for the full sample, and K-band data for a limited subset of the sample.  We hope to obtain the rest of the K-band measurements, as well as observations in Ka- and Q-bands in 2026A.  ALMA will provide measurements in two settings in each of Bands 3 and 4.

More information (including links to calibrated data) will be provided here when the project is in a more completed state.

The target sample is available [here](https://seanandrews.github.io/files/VLA_targets.txt).
